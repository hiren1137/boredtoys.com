## How To Play the Stimulation Clicker

You start with one big red button and a lot of calm. Click it. Each click gives you stimulation points, and the counter at the top shows how many you have.

Points are for spending. The shop lists the next few upgrades and their prices. When you can afford one, it turns green. Buy it and something new joins the screen: a bouncing square, a news ticker, a robot hand that clicks for you, a beat, pop-up messages, a tiny video game and much more. Upgrades you cannot see yet show as question marks until you get close to their price.

Most upgrades also earn points on their own. Some add points every second, some make each click worth more, and some pay out every time a shape hits a wall. A few things on screen can be tapped for a bonus, like notifications and the pinata.

When you own every upgrade, one last item appears: Find Inner Peace. Buy it and everything goes quiet.

You can click with a mouse, tap with your finger, or use the keyboard. Press Tab to reach the button and press Space or Enter to click.

## What Your Score Means

Your score is how long it took to go from your first click to inner peace. Lower is better. Your fastest time is saved as your personal best.

- **Under 10 minutes:** you shop like a pro and never let points sit around.
- **10 to 15 minutes:** a solid, steady run with plenty of time to enjoy the mess.
- **Over 15 minutes:** you stopped to watch the logo, read the headlines or whack every pinata. Honestly, fair.

The result screen also shows the total stimulation you earned and how many times you clicked yourself. The robot hand's clicks do not count toward your click total.

## Tips To Improve

- **Spend fast.** Points sitting in your counter do nothing. Buy the cheapest upgrade as soon as it lights up.
- **Grab the robot hand early.** Free clicks add up, and the turbo version later makes a big difference.
- **Tap the notifications.** Each one pays several seconds of income, which is great when you are saving for something big.
- **Smash the pinata.** It needs three hits and disappears after a few seconds, so keep an eye out.
- **Watch the corners.** When the bouncing logo hits a corner exactly, you get a jackpot worth fifty normal bounces.

## Why Clicker Games Feel So Good

Clicker games, also called incremental games, are built around one simple loop. You do a small action, a number goes up, and you spend that number to make it go up faster. Each new upgrade comes along just as the last one starts to feel slow.

This version adds a twist. Every upgrade makes the screen busier, so the louder it gets, the closer you are to the quiet ending. It is a silly way to notice how much stuff can compete for your attention at once, and how nice it feels when it all stops.
